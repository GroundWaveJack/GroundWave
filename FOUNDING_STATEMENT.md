# Founding statement

> “If I can leave nothing else to humanity but a memory of my name and a thing I created, this would be a really cool one. Communication is an essential human necessity, and it’s becoming increasingly difficult to have any control over it. It’s time to stop making communication a commodity. It should be as close to impossible to prevent as we can make it. Near or far, fast or slow, smooth or choppy, the system should do its best to give you the ability to securely communicate with whomever you’re trying to reach, no matter the means. Anything. Anyone. Anywhere. Best effort.”
>
> — Jack, 2026

This is a founding motivation, not a technical guarantee. “Best effort” is bounded by available paths, physics, law, safety, consent, privacy, resource ownership, and explicit policy.
