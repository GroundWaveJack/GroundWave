# Architectural doctrine

These are adopted design constraints, not implementation claims. Changes require an architecture decision record or request for comments.

1. Harriet is not one protocol.
2. Reticulum is an adopted profile target under design, not the platform.
3. The connective layer preserves declared intent constraints, identity bindings, policy, provenance, and security—not arbitrary human meaning.
4. Ordinary relays must not require plaintext. Plaintext carriage or access is explicit, visible, policy-authorized, and profile-constrained.
5. Only explicitly trusted gateways translate carriage or representation.
6. Lossy translation is declared, attributable, and auditable; unsupported semantics fail explicitly.
7. Original retention follows explicit content, deletion, privacy, safety, owner, and storage policy. It is never presumed.
8. Harriet owns only the missing connective semantics, profiles, and conformance layer.
9. Solved layers use open standards and mature open-source projects.
10. Contribute upstream before forking.
11. Competing standards are supported through profiles and adapters rather than forced equivalence.
12. No walled garden or required centralized service.
13. No vendor, carrier, cloud, radio, package, or application is mandatory.
14. Hardware embodiments are replaceable reference designs.
15. Graceful degradation and explicit refusal are mandatory.
16. Best effort does not authorize flooding, surveillance, unsafe behavior, or policy downgrade.
17. Privacy, resource limits, regulation, airtime fairness, and owner sovereignty are hard constraints.
18. Reachability metadata is tracking-capable and receives privacy protection regardless of purpose.
19. Promotion assigns bounded work, never plaintext or governance authority.
20. Future ambition never appears as present implementation.
21. Ordinary participation must not require command-line expertise or networking knowledge; simple human preferences map to technical profiles underneath.
22. The Harriet name carries the attribution and stewardship obligations in `WHY_HARRIET.md`; historical struggle is never product decoration.
