# Code of conduct

Groundwave is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous community where pseudonymous participation is first-class.

Be constructive, respect boundaries, separate people from technical claims, disclose conflicts, credit work, and make room for different experience levels and access needs. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, impersonation, deliberate privacy invasion, and encouragement of unsafe or unlawful operation are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately using the least-sensitive channel available; if no private channel exists, open a minimal issue requesting one without naming people or describing the incident. Do not use vulnerability reporting unless the report also concerns a vulnerability. Jack currently handles reports and may remove content or restrict participation. Confidentiality is best effort, conflicts will be disclosed where safe, and no independent appeal body exists yet. This limitation will be revisited as maintainership grows.
