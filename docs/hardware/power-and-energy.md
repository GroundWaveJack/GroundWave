# Power and energy

**State:** pre-qualification requirements under design; no embodiment currently publishes a complete evidence set

Every embodiment publishes operating modes, measured consumption distributions, peak current, cutoff behavior, charge sources, battery chemistry, thermal limits, and safe failure behavior. Planners may use energy budgets but cannot override owner reserves.

Solar autonomy and runtime are environmental test results, not component-derived guarantees. Batteries require protection, fusing, qualified charging, transport guidance, replaceability policy, and end-of-life handling.

Power measurements use a declared configuration, workload, radio duty cycle, battery condition, temperature range, instrument, sample interval, and duration. Report distributions and worst observed transients rather than a single typical value. Characterize startup, transmit, compute peak, idle, sleep, charging, shutdown, brownout, and fault states.

Thermal qualification measures component, battery, enclosure, connector, and user-contact temperatures at defined ambient conditions and orientations. It verifies derating, charge inhibition, controlled shutdown, restart hysteresis, blocked-vent behavior where applicable, and fault containment. Estimated dissipation or a successful bench boot is not thermal evidence.
