Candidate embodiments include Zack handhelds, pocket or wearable communicators, mobile/vehicle/fixed Bases, expedition systems, ship or aircraft installations, remote outposts, ambient plug-anywhere nodes, router-resident nodes, and phone or desktop edges. Each embodiment needs stated maturity, hazards, owner controls, regulatory region, acceptance tests, and replaceable component classes. Exact SKUs belong outside requirements.
