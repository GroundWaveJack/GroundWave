# Neighbor federation

**State:** Architecture under design; not implemented.

Personal and household clusters may exchange bounded capability, inventory, and routing summaries with neighboring administrative domains. Owners set storage, airtime, bandwidth, energy, and schedule budgets. Admission control and proof of resource cost may mitigate Sybil and exhaustion attacks without centralized social scoring.

Compact reconciliation and Bloom filters are evaluation candidates, with false positives and inventory leakage measured. Federation does not imply shared trust or unrestricted transit.
