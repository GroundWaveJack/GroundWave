# Segmentation and scale

A city must not become one broadcast domain. The proposed hierarchy is personal cluster, household cluster, building or block segment, neighborhood segment, regional relay, and long-haul bridge. Areas, route summarization, policy boundaries, congestion control, and scheduled exchange limit state and airtime.

Dynamic promotion assigns bounded work based on capability and owner policy. It confers no plaintext access or social authority. Scale claims require simulation, adversarial tests, and staged deployments; none exist today.
