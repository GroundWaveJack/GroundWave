# Segmentation and scale

**State:** Architecture under design; not implemented.

A city must not become one broadcast domain. A candidate hierarchy is personal cluster, household cluster, building or block segment, neighborhood segment, regional relay, and long-haul bridge. This is an evaluation model, not an adopted topology. Areas, route summarization, policy boundaries, congestion control, and scheduled exchange are conventional tools to evaluate at the layer that owns routing.

Dynamic promotion assigns bounded work based on capability and owner policy. It confers no plaintext access or social authority. Scale claims require simulation, adversarial tests, and staged deployments; none exist today.
