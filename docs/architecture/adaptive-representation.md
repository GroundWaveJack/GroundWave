# Adaptive representation

**State:** Architecture under design; not implemented.

Representations form a policy-constrained set, not a universal quality ladder. Video might permit audio, an image, text, or an authenticated status bit, but only when the sender authorizes those derivations and the recipient can interpret them.

Selection weighs deadline, capability, bandwidth, energy, storage, trust, and expected delivery—not aesthetic quality alone. Semantic fidelity cannot always be measured automatically, so uncertain content must be declared and human review may be required.
