# Home relays and reachability

A home relay is a declared relationship authorizing bounded rendezvous, cache, or forwarding behavior. The platform may exchange recipient reachability hints, last-contact observations, relay affinity, and expiring delivery-likelihood estimates.

These records are **tracking-capable metadata**, even when tracking is not their purpose. Repeated last-contact observations, stable relay affinities, timing, and home-relay declarations can reveal routines, relationships, or location. Naming them reachability does not remove that risk.

They must be minimized, access-controlled, scoped, revocable, and short-lived. Promotion gives a relay more work, never more plaintext or authority.
