# HF digital

**State:** research horizon; not implemented

High frequency (HF) radio may provide low-rate, long-haul, scheduled, or uncertain contacts through external radios and established digital modes. Groundwave should profile externally controlled carriage interfaces, not invent a waveform. No mode, radio interface, or operating profile has been selected.

Licensing, band plans, operator control, station identification, duty cycle, propagation, encryption restrictions, interference, and emergency traffic rules vary by jurisdiction and require authoritative external verification. Any future automatic transmission must be disabled unless a deployment profile establishes lawful authority, owner consent, resource limits, and a tested fail-safe.
