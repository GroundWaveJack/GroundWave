# HF digital

**State:** research integration

HF may provide low-rate, long-haul, scheduled or uncertain contacts through external radios and established digital modes. Groundwave should integrate radio control and carriage interfaces, not invent a waveform.

Licensing, band plans, operator control, station identification, duty cycle, propagation, encryption restrictions, interference, and emergency traffic rules vary by jurisdiction. Automatic transmission remains disabled until a deployment profile establishes authority.
