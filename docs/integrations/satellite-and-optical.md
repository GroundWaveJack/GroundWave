# Satellite and optical convergence

**State:** research horizon; not implemented

Future adapters may use third-party satellite or optical links when available and policy permits. Groundwave does not claim independent access, global coverage, anti-jam capability, or infrastructure independence for such links.

Research profiles must address provider dependency, contact schedules, pointing, weather, link budgets, regulation, metadata, cost, custody, and failure modes. Available interfaces, standards, permission, and provider policies require external verification. Groundwave would own only the communication-envelope mapping and adapter evidence; it should not build launch, network, modem, or optical-link infrastructure.
