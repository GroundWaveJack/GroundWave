# FieldNode specification v0.1 — superseded historical draft

**Status:** superseded and unqualified. Do not use this file as build, procurement, safety, regulatory, or deployment guidance.

The April 2026 draft mixed hardware requirements, commercial part selections, cost estimates, operational instructions, and qualification targets before any prototype or published measurement campaign existed. Claims about weather resistance, solar autonomy, runtime, cost, radio legality, range, build time, and component suitability were design goals rather than demonstrated results.

The original is preserved verbatim at [docs/project/archive/FIELDNODE_SPEC_v0.md](docs/project/archive/FIELDNODE_SPEC_v0.md). Current platform-level hardware rules are in the [hardware documentation](docs/hardware/embodiments.md) and [qualification model](docs/hardware/qualification-model.md). Future FieldNode work belongs in its satellite repository and must separate a supplier-neutral Hardware Requirements Specification, exact evidence-backed Reference Qualified BOM, and time-sensitive Procurement Manifest.
