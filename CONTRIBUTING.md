# Contributing

Harriet welcomes pseudonymous contributors and expects contributors to follow the attribution and stewardship commitments in [WHY_HARRIET.md](WHY_HARRIET.md). Do not include legal names, private contact data, credentials, precise private deployments, or unnecessary radio identifiers. Contributions are reviewed on technical merit and conduct.

Issues, reviews, measurements, design discussion, test evidence, and draft patches are welcome. Open an issue or RFC before substantial new architecture, protocols, repositories, or hardware requirements.

Use the issue chooser to report a defect, submit hardware evidence, propose an integration profile, or describe a documentation/user need. Describe the task and constraint without prescribing a protocol. Security and conduct reports follow their private routes and must not contain sensitive details in a public issue.

Canonical licenses, file boundaries, and contribution terms are unresolved. Until they are adopted through the required governance process, third-party reusable code, hardware source, and documentation must not be merged. Draft patches may still be shared for discussion and review, but maintainers must not treat them as accepted reusable project source. Contributors must not assume [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md) grants reuse rights. This task does not select or alter a license.

Pull requests must state maturity, what exists versus what is proposed, security/privacy effects, tests, external standards affected, and any AI assistance that requires human verification.

Use RFCs to discuss proposals and ADRs to record accepted architecture decisions; neither substitutes for implementation or test evidence. Follow the [decision lifecycle](docs/project/decision-log.md). Keep commits coherent, do not push to `main`, and run the [documented checks](docs/development/testing-strategy.md). Hardware evidence includes configuration, instrumentation, environment, procedure, raw results, uncertainty, and failures.

See the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), and [security process](SECURITY.md).
